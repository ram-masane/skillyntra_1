# 3-Minute Judge Demo
## 1. Labour Market Pulse
Select Pune + Manufacturing.
Show emerging roles and top skills.

Say:
"Skillyntra starts with the demand side. Instead of asking only how many jobs
exist, it identifies which skills repeatedly appear in demand and whether
the signal is supported by additional evidence."

## 2. Student Skill Gap
Choose Predictive Maintenance Engineer.
Current skills: Python, SQL, Basic Manufacturing.

Show missing IoT, Machine Learning, Predictive Maintenance, etc.

Say:
"We now move from the market to the individual. Skillyntra compares the
target role requirements with the student's current profile and converts
an abstract skill mismatch into prioritized gaps."

## 3. Curriculum Alignment
Open the curriculum page.
Show Industrial IoT / Data Analytics / Predictive Maintenance.

Say:
"The same intelligence is useful at institute level. We compare course
coverage with industry demand and identify missing or under-covered skills."

## 4. District Planner
Select Pune.
Show demand vs capacity and shortage.
Click "Download district training plan (CSV)".

Say:
"Finally, Skillyntra turns this intelligence into planning. A district with
higher demand than training capacity becomes a priority for capacity
expansion — and the plan can be exported directly for department use."

## 5. Emerging Trends & Flagged Courses (Intelligence page)
Show the sector growth chart (Rising / Stable / Declining) and the Flagged
Courses table.

Say:
"We don't just track today's demand — sector growth signals flag which
industries are rising or declining, and every course is automatically
scored against demand alignment, placement outcomes and sector trend.
That's how we surface courses that need curriculum review versus ones
where training capacity may be oversupplied, exactly as the problem
statement asks for."

## 6. Employer Validation (Evidence page)
Pick a skill, set a relevance score, submit.

Say:
"Employers can directly validate how relevant a skill is right now — this
feeds straight back into the evidence score, closing the loop between
what employers say and what the platform recommends."

## 7. Close
"That creates our industry-to-training-to-student feedback loop —
demand and proficiency level all the way through to district-level
training plans."
